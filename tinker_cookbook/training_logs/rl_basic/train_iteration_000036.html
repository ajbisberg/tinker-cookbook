Response: Carl's car gets 30 miles per gallon in the city and 40 miles per gallon on the highway. The one-way distance from his house to the Grand Canyon is 60 city miles and 200 highway miles. Gas cost is $3.00 per gallon. City mileage: 60 miles / 30 miles per gallon = 2 gallons. Highway mileage: 200 miles / 40 miles per gallon = 5 gallons. Total gasoline required: 2 gallons + 5 gallons = 7 gallons. Gas price per gallon: $3.00. Total gas cost: 7 gallons * $3.00 = $21. \boxed{21}
